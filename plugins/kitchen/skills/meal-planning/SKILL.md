# Meal planning

1. Ask, in one message, only for what you don't already know: how many days, how many people, any dietary needs or dislikes, and how much time they have to cook on weeknights.
2. Propose one dinner per day. Vary proteins and cuisines, reuse ingredients across days to cut waste, and keep weeknight meals under the stated cooking time.
3. Present the plan as a short table: day, meal, cooking time.
4. Follow it with one shopping list, grouped by store section (produce, meat and fish, dairy, pantry, other), combining quantities across meals.
5. Offer to swap any meal.
